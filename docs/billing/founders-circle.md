# Pricing and early access

The former tier-based Founders Circle copy is retired. Current commercial terms are defined on the public [pricing page](https://www.coralledger.com/pricing) and measured per VAT registration (one nine-digit TIN).

## Current terms

- Standard active-taxpayer price: **B$199/month**.
- The first 25 activated taxpayers receive **B$59/month while active**.
- A deregistered taxpayer remains **read-only and exportable at B$19/month** from the next billing month.
- A qualifying paired referral gives both active taxpayers **up to B$20/month off**, for up to five pairs, never below B$59/month.
- Open-beta access is free while the beta is running; the public pricing page controls current transition language.

These terms replace the retired claims about a 20-member Founders Circle, firm-wide client caps, and a B$99/B$499 tier comparison. Do not use those retired values in customer guidance.
